# PCB Fabrication Houses Guide

A comprehensive guide to selecting PCB (Printed Circuit Board) fabrication manufacturers for various needs - from hobby projects to professional audio electronics.

## Quick Recommendations

### For Hobbyists & Small Projects
- **JLCPCB** - Best overall value, extremely affordable
- **PCBWay** - Great support and flexibility
- **OSH Park** - Best for US-based small batches with premium quality

### For Professional/Audio Electronics
- **Advanced Circuits** - US-based, fast turnaround
- **Sierra Circuits** - High-quality, professional-grade
- **TTM Technologies** - Industry leader for complex designs

---

## Budget-Friendly Options (Hobby & Prototyping)

### JLCPCB
- **Website:** [https://jlcpcb.com/](https://jlcpcb.com/)
- **Pricing:** As low as $2-$5 for 10 boards
- **Turnaround:** 24 hours available
- **Best For:** Low-cost prototypes, fast iterations
- **Pros:**
  - Extremely affordable for small quantities
  - Quick production times
  - PCB assembly and stencils available
  - Good for low-to-medium complexity designs
- **Cons:**
  - Shipping costs can add up for international orders
  - Based in China (longer shipping to US)

### PCBWay
- **Website:** [https://www.pcbway.com/](https://www.pcbway.com/)
- **Pricing:** Competitive with JLCPCB
- **Turnaround:** 24-48 hours available
- **Best For:** Projects needing good support and various services
- **Pros:**
  - Excellent customer support
  - Offers assembly, flex/rigid-flex, stencils, 3D printing, CNC
  - Good quality for the price
  - Responsive service
- **Cons:**
  - Slightly more expensive than JLCPCB for smallest orders
  - International shipping costs

### OSH Park
- **Website:** [https://oshpark.com/](https://oshpark.com/)
- **Pricing:** Per square inch ($5/sq.in for 2-layer)
- **Turnaround:** 9-12 days
- **Best For:** Small US-based orders, premium finish
- **Pros:**
  - Distinctive purple solder mask
  - High-quality ENIG (gold) finish
  - Free US domestic shipping
  - Simple pricing model
  - Great for small or unusual-shaped PCBs
- **Cons:**
  - More expensive for larger boards
  - Slower turnaround than Asian manufacturers

### Other Budget Options
- **Seeed Studio Fusion** - Similar to JLCPCB/PCBWay
- **Elecrow** - Good for prototyping with special deals
- **ALLPCB** - Competitive pricing with good hobbyist support

## Professional-Grade Manufacturers (USA)

### Advanced Circuits (APCT)
- **Best For:** US-based rapid prototyping, professional projects
- **Pros:**
  - Industry-leading turnaround times
  - ITAR and MIL-PRF-31032 certified
  - FreeDFM online tools
  - US-based manufacturing
- **Cons:**
  - Higher cost than Asian manufacturers

### TTM Technologies
- **Best For:** Complex, high-reliability boards
- **Pros:**
  - Industry leader for aerospace, defense, medical
  - HDI, rigid-flex, RF/microwave capabilities
  - ISO and AS9100 certified
  - Sustainability focus
- **Cons:**
  - Premium pricing
  - Geared toward high-volume professional use

### Sanmina Corporation
- **Best For:** Full-spectrum electronics manufacturing
- **Pros:**
  - Rapid prototyping to high-volume
  - Military and medical standards
  - Global presence
- **Cons:**
  - Professional-level pricing

### Sierra Circuits
- **Best For:** High-quality professional prototypes
- **Pros:**
  - Excellent quality control
  - Fast delivery
  - Great engineering support
- **Cons:**
  - Higher pricing than hobbyist options

### Jabil
- **Best For:** Complex multilayer and HDI boards
- **Pros:**
  - Expert in miniaturized builds
  - Design-to-manufacturing integration
  - Global supply chain
- **Cons:**
  - Geared toward larger operations

## How to Choose

### Consider These Factors:

1. **Budget**
   - Hobby/Prototyping: JLCPCB, PCBWay
   - Professional: Advanced Circuits, Sierra Circuits

2. **Location**
   - US-based: OSH Park, Advanced Circuits, Sierra Circuits
   - International: JLCPCB, PCBWay

3. **Turnaround Time**
   - Fastest: JLCPCB, PCBWay (24 hours)
   - Standard: OSH Park (9-12 days)

4. **Quality Requirements**
   - Premium: OSH Park, Sierra Circuits
   - Good Value: JLCPCB, PCBWay
   - Mission-Critical: TTM Technologies, Sanmina

5. **Complexity**
   - Simple 2-4 layer: Any manufacturer
   - HDI/Flex/Rigid-Flex: TTM Technologies, Jabil, PCBWay
   - RF/Microwave: TTM Technologies, Advanced Circuits

6. **Certifications Needed**
   - Military/Aerospace: TTM Technologies, Advanced Circuits
   - Medical: Sanmina, TTM Technologies
   - ITAR: Advanced Circuits

7. **Additional Services**
   - PCB Assembly: JLCPCB, PCBWay
   - Stencils: JLCPCB, PCBWay
   - 3D Printing/CNC: PCBWay

## For Audio Electronics Projects

When building custom audio gear, consider:

- **Prototyping:** Start with JLCPCB or PCBWay for cost-effective iterations
- **Small Batches:** OSH Park provides excellent quality for boutique runs
- **Professional Products:** Advanced Circuits or Sierra Circuits for commercial audio equipment
- **High-End/Audiophile:** Consider TTM Technologies for complex analog designs with tight tolerances

**Tip:** Most audio electronics don't require extreme complexity, so budget manufacturers work well for most projects. Reserve professional manufacturers for final production runs or critical RF/analog designs.

---

## Quality Checklist

Before selecting a manufacturer, verify:
- [ ] IPC standards compliance
- [ ] ISO 9001 certification (for professional use)
- [ ] DFM (Design for Manufacturing) support
- [ ] Testing capabilities (AOI, X-ray, electrical testing)
- [ ] Customer reviews and reputation
- [ ] Clear pricing and shipping costs
- [ ] Technical support availability

---

## Conclusion

**For most hobby electronics and audio prototyping:** Start with **JLCPCB** for the best value.

**For US-based small batches with premium quality:** Choose **OSH Park**.

**For professional/commercial audio electronics:** Go with **Advanced Circuits** or **Sierra Circuits**.

**For complex, high-reliability designs:** Select **TTM Technologies** or **Sanmina**.

Always verify current pricing, lead times, and capabilities before placing an order, as these can change over time.
