Before You Send Files

What should I send?

Clean stems, same start point, no clipping on the mix bus, and a rough mix if you have one.

How many revisions?

Two revision rounds on the starter mix. One revision round on the intro rate.

How fast is turnaround?

Five business days for the starter mix. Larger projects are quoted separately.

Can I still book local sessions?

Yes. Dirt Cat still works locally in North Arkansas and Southern Missouri by request.
